The House

Three generations, one open kitchen.

Maison Auré began as a nineteen-seat bistro run by a butcher's daughter with a taste for Burgundy and no patience for shortcuts. Today the kitchen still opens onto the dining room, the stock still simmers for two days, and the wine list is still chosen by hand, bottle by bottle, from growers we've known for decades.

We cook the way our grandmothers taught us — with butter, with patience, and with the conviction that a good meal should slow the whole evening down.
